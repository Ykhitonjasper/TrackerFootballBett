Match Journal is a local companion: a fixture slate, editorial match predictions with a confidence level, live scores, and a personal watchlist. Predictions are personal research notes. They are not financial advice and the app does not pay out based on results.

The app does not take payments, hold a balance, or pay anyone out. Upcoming features listed on the updates page stay inside that same product: more research tools, not a payments product.

A display name and favorite sport are stored on the iPhone. There is no online profile. If you delete the app or use Delete all data, that local store goes away.

Accuracy

Scores, timelines, and predictions are provided as-is. Live clocks can drift. Editorial forecasts can miss. Do your own research.
