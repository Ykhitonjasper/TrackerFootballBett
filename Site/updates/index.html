Product notes

Upcoming updates

Match Journal 1.0 is predictions plus live scores. Everything below is planned work — not a promise of a date, and not a hidden extra already in the binary.

Shipping now · 1.0

Daily written forecasts, confidence pips, live and upcoming fixtures across six sports, match timelines and stats, a personal watchlist, and a local display name. Reset and delete-all live in Settings.

Next up

Kickoff reminders. Optional local notifications when a starred match or an open prediction is close to starting. No push server.

Form strips. Last-five results and a short head-to-head line under the prediction note, so the write-up is easier to judge.

Settled archive. A cleaner filter for landed / missed / void with sport chips, so you can review past notes without scrolling the whole slate.

Later

More competitions. Extra cups and secondary leagues, still stored on-device.

Home Screen widget. Today’s top forecast without opening the app.

iPad layout. Two-column layout: slate on one side, prediction note on the other.

What will not show up

Match Journal will not add an online profile, payments, or a way to place anything through the app. Predictions stay editorial research notes.
