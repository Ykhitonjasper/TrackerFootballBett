Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Match Journal iOS app. The app is a local sports companion: match predictions, live scores, and a watchlist.

What stays on the iPhone

The app stores a display name, a favorite sport, fixture data, editorial predictions, a watchlist of fixture IDs, and simple preferences (sounds, local alerts toggle). That data never leaves the device. There is no Match Journal server for profiles.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect email, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, payment details, or advertising IDs. It does not run analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Why UserDefaults appears in the privacy manifest

The app uses UserDefaults on-device to remember onboarding completion, the watchlist, and a seed flag. That is the CA92.1 reason in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy. It is not sent anywhere.

This website

These pages are static. They do not set marketing cookies and they do not have a form that asks for personal information.

Delete

Settings includes Delete all data. That clears fixtures, predictions, the watchlist, and the local profile, then returns you to onboarding. You can also delete the app, which removes the on-device store.

Children

The product is meant for sports fans aged 12 and up. It is not directed at young children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Support link in the app, which opens this site.
